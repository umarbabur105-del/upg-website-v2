# UPG Backlink Outreach Drafts

Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

These are approval-gated drafts. They must be personalized from the prospect's current editorial or membership rules. Do not send them in bulk, request an optimized anchor, offer payment for a ranking link, or claim results UPG cannot prove.

Five researched, publication-specific drafts and human-author briefs are maintained in `docs/backlink-personalized-drafts.md`. They remain unsent until Umar approves each exact email.

## Personalization checklist

Before any send:

1. Read the current submission or membership rules.
2. Name one specific editor, page, audience, or directory category.
3. Choose one UPG asset that solves a real reader problem.
4. Remove every unsupported technical, sustainability, performance, or market claim.
5. Confirm image rights. AI concepts are not customer case studies.
6. Ask for editorial consideration, feedback, or eligibility; never demand a backlink.
7. Record prospect, date, draft, response, live URL, target page, and relationship in the CRM or outreach tracker.

## Rejected tactics

- Paid dofollow links
- Automated directory blasts
- Reciprocal-link pages
- Guest-post networks created for anchors
- Private blog networks
- Reusing a customer project without permission
- Submitting AI-generated concepts as completed production work
- Awards or association logos before approval
