# Commercial product guides

Reviewed: 2026-09-27

## Buyer flow

The existing custom mailer and collapsible magnetic product pages now carry product-specific pricing factors, a 250-unit planning MOQ explanation, quantity-break requests, annotated configuration concepts, and buyer FAQs. The product, selected quantity and configuration context continue into the existing quote form.

Buyers can request 250, 500 or 1,000 units individually, or request a same-specification comparison in one enquiry. The comparison starts at 250 units and places the alternative quantities and freight clarification request in the visible starting brief. These are enquiry shortcuts, not published price tiers.

## Commercial boundaries

Pricing follows the existing commercial terms. Different sizes or artworks cannot be assumed to combine into one MOQ. Production, sample/proof arrangements, assembly, freight, duties and taxes are governed by the accepted written quote.

Mailer scope remains custom corrugated ear-lock presentation packaging. Standard shipping cartons, master cartons and RSC cases remain excluded. Collapsible boxes carry no fixed freight-saving or assembly-service promise.

Current gallery assets are generated concepts, not customer case studies or production photographs. The owner subsequently requested neutral, product-focused customer copy without image-provenance disclaimers. Gallery captions now describe configuration options; no customer-project claims are attached to those visuals. Rights-cleared production photographs and verified project facts remain needed before naming genuine case studies.
