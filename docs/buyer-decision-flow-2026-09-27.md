# Buyer decisions and quote context — 2026-09-27

## Outcome and flow

The commercial guide explains what changes price, while the application and task guides explain how to prepare a packaging project. This batch connects those decisions to the existing quote form:

- Compare quotes against one specification, then request review with that comparison context.
- Choose a PR or subscription application, then preserve its product, style and intended use in the enquiry.
- Choose the next task in the production and proof/sample guides, then follow the relevant guide or request a project-specific timing or approval-path review.
- Avoid late artwork changes, rushed approval and measurement mistakes through practical prevention checks in the existing production, proof and measuring guides.

The form keeps the starting brief separately from the buyer's additional notes. No new form submission flow, CRM column, API contract, tool or landing page is introduced.

## Content boundaries

The existing 250-unit planning minimum and quote-led commercial terms remain the source of truth. Comparison guidance does not offer price matching, invent a rate card, or promise availability or production timing. The fixed-target-date path requests a feasibility review; the proof/sample path requests confirmation for the specific project.

UPG is a new company. The founder supplied six years of packaging experience as the basis for practical buyer guidance; this does not establish six years of UPG trading history. The experience is presented as advice, not a UPG customer case or testimonial. No customer identity, commercial result or production photograph is invented. The public site does not receive image-provenance disclaimers.
