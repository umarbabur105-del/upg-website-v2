## What this does and does not prove

The technical acceptance checks are all five rendered buying guides, matching FAQ markup, valid gallery image mappings, quantity/context preservation at quote handoff, consistent catalog and text references, build, lint, rendered SEO audit and live checks.

Search indexing, citations, referrals and qualified enquiries are separate outcomes. A successful deployment or accepted IndexNow notification does not prove them. Private analytics and CRM evidence belong outside this public repository.

Google states that ordinary SEO remains the basis for AI Search eligibility and requires no special AI markup or files. OpenAI distinguishes OAI-SearchBot search access from GPTBot training controls. UPG's discovery files are supplementary references, not a ranking guarantee.
