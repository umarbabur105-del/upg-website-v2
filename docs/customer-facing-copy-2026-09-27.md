# Customer-facing packaging copy

Reviewed: 2026-09-27

## Direction

Product pages should help buyers understand the format, customization choices, quantity and next step. Customer-facing hero captions, galleries, FAQs and quote-prefill notes use direct product language. Image-provenance warnings and internal review commentary have been removed from those surfaces. Product images are not described as completed customer jobs.

The 250-unit planning MOQ, product scope, project pricing and fixed-price sample-kit contracts remain in place. Commercial details stay in relevant pricing answers and terms instead of repeating beneath product images.

## Official-site benchmarks

- [Teal Packaging](https://tealpackaging.com/): direct product categories, uses and quote actions.
- [Print247](https://print247.us/): product benefits, finish options and a clear quote handoff.
- [PackMojo](https://packmojo.com/custom-packaging/tray-and-sleeve-boxes/): sizes, quantities, configuration and sampling choices.
- [Packlane](https://packlane.com/products/mailer-box): product configuration and direct design actions.
- [PakFactory](https://pakfactory.com/custom-rigid-setup-boxes.html): packaging structures and customization framed around product use.
- [noissue](https://noissue.co/): product categories with ordering details and quote routes; official support explains available quantity choices.

These are tone and organization references. Competitor prices, turnaround, certifications, shipping offers, customer results and service commitments are not UPG facts.

## Changes

Product galleries describe printing, inserts, finishes and structures. Quantity comparisons remain available. Enquiry notes carry the buyer's requested configuration without image commentary. Industry, application and blog image captions and alt text follow the same product language. Public discovery descriptions are aligned. The old SEO gate requiring blog image disclaimers now checks that the rejected copy is absent; MOQ validation distinguishes true minimum claims from legitimate comparison quantities.
