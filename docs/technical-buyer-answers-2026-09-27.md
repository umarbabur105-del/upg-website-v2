## Commercial authority

The owner explicitly confirmed UPG's general policy that CMYK and two PMS colors use the same base print-pricing basis for an otherwise unchanged specification. This is UPG's commercial policy, not a technical equivalence between color systems. Four spot colors or mixed CMYK-plus-spot specifications require their own price confirmation.

An artwork change can create a separate print version even when the dieline is unchanged. In conventional offset production, changed color separations can require revised plates; the run plan and quotation must account for versions and their quantities. Do not imply that every change necessarily replaces every plate or forbids combined production planning.
