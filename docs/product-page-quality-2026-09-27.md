# Core product buyer content

Scope: the four existing non-mailer product pages. Mailer boxes retain first position and their separately confirmed offer. No new product family, indexable URL or quote-form field is introduced.

## Search intent and page ownership

| Canonical product | Main intent | Supporting buyer questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/products/custom-tuck-boxes` | Custom printed tuck boxes / folding cartons | Straight vs reverse tuck; auto-lock vs interlock base; white SBS vs kraft; PT thickness; artwork versions |
| `/products/custom-magnetic-boxes` | Custom magnetic closure boxes / rigid gift boxes | Assembled vs collapsible; fitted inserts; internal dimensions; inside printing; transit protection |
| `/products/custom-collapsible-magnetic-boxes` | Custom collapsible / fold-flat magnetic boxes | Assembly workflow; insert packing; usable interior; complete shipment cost; finishes |
| `/products/custom-mylar-bags` | Custom printed Mylar bags / pouches | Stand-up vs flat-bottom vs flat pouches; fill capacity; zipper vs heat seal; film performance; finished pouches vs rollstock |

These are intent choices supported by the live product scope and supplier terminology, not measured keyword-volume or ranking claims. Existing style guides retain the narrower format queries. Metadata is supported by visible product-specific explanations rather than a keyword list alone.

## Research basis

Primary supplier pages reviewed September 27, 2026:

- [PackMojo folding cartons](https://packmojo.com/custom-packaging/folding-carton-boxes/): flap orientation, pre-glued auto bottom, manually assembled locking base and print surfaces.
- [Teal tuck boxes](https://tealpackaging.com/tuck-boxes/): dieline orientation, packing workflow and connected structure decisions.
- [PackMojo luxury box comparison](https://packmojo.com/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-luxury-box/): assembled rigid versus collapsible structure.
- [PakFactory custom pouches](https://pakfactory.com/custom-pouches.html) and [pouch guide](https://pakfactory.com/blog/custom-pouch-packaging-master-guide/): pouch formats and form-fill-seal rollstock distinction.
- [ePac stand-up pouches](https://epacflexibles.com/products/stand-up-pouches/): width/height/gusset, closures, film selection and filling workflow.
- [Packaging HQ sizing guide](https://packaginghq.com/resources/guides/pouch-sizing-guide): fill weight, product density, usable volume and sample-based sizing.

Copy is original and limited to supported UPG formats. Competitor lead times, prices, certifications, performance limits, environmental claims and customer examples are not UPG offer evidence.

Owner-confirmed policy: shipping cost is included in the quoted price for the agreed destination/service; duties and taxes are specified in the quote. Mailer production and artwork-service terms do not extend to other families. Finished pouch quantity choices do not establish a rollstock MOQ.
